About this preview

What is real, what is simulated, and what is not validated.

DATA PATH

Seeded synthetic entities produce simulated sensor observations. One positional modality (radar) is passed to a constant-velocity tracker, whose estimated tracks are what this screen displays. Ground truth is never used for association or display.

LIMITATIONS

This is a small tracking baseline demonstrated on synthetic observations. It is not production tracking, not validated threat classification, and not multi-sensor fusion. Attention levels are scenario-awareness rules applied to estimated geometry; they are not evidence of hostile intent, and nothing here infers a weapon, an attacker or a control mode. Altitude is a simulation value in the local frame, not height above ground.

ATTENTION THRESHOLDS

Demonstration values, chosen for legibility, not from any operational standard: inside the monitored radius is high priority; closing faster than 2 m/s with under 45 s to the boundary is high priority; a level must hold for 3 s before the display changes, so noise does not make the list reorder.
